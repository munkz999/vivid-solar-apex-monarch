Support

Bag Chart · Tyler Tamburin

Bag Chart helps you chart carry and total distances for your golf bag, adjust for swing speed and loft, and optionally factor in conditions.

How to get help

  1. Open the app and tap the flag (menu) control.
  2. Choose Feedback.
  3. Pick a category, write your message, and submit.

Your note is emailed to the developer via a form service. We typically reply as soon as we can.

Pro / Unlock

Pro features are unlocked with a one-time In-App Purchase through Apple (not a subscription). Restore purchases from the unlock flow if you reinstall or switch devices using the same Apple ID.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for how Bag Chart handles on-device data, optional weather, feedback, and purchases.
